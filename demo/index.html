Internal communication

Share through contacts and email

In the real Records app, sharing is handled through an internal communication workflow tied to the library contact list.

Admins can keep contacts for board members, staff, partner organizations, or other recipients, then send a message by email with the selected document or generated PDF attached.

The contact list keeps recurring recipients organized, while the document stays controlled by the library's access and public/private settings.
