Reduce manual work.

Utility teams often juggle QuickBooks, spreadsheets, paper files, Word documents, email, mailed bills, payment portals, and handwritten notes. N3XRA Utilities brings that work into one integrated system built to support growing operations.

Platform scope

Built to support the whole utility relationship.

N3XRA Utilities is designed to give each provider a branded customer hub, a practical staff workspace, and a configurable operations layer that can grow as billing, payments, meter data, outage notices, documents, and service workflows come online.

Customer portal

Give customers one place to start.

Account access, service addresses, support requests, document uploads, announcements, outage alerts, notification preferences, FAQs, and service start, stop, or transfer requests.

Staff workspace

Keep daily work organized.

Customer search, request inboxes, ticket assignment, status updates, internal notes, document review, announcement publishing, reporting, exports, and staff role management.

Configurable forms

Collect the right information upfront.

Flexible forms for new service, outage reports, leak reports, payment assistance, construction requests, commercial service, account updates, complaints, and general inquiries.

Provider setup

Make each utility feel like its own system.

Company branding, logos, colors, service types, contact details, support categories, required documents, business hours, emergency numbers, Stripe Connect setup, and public pages.

Modular features

Turn on what each provider needs.

Billing links, payments, outage reporting, meter readings, permits, inspections, rebates, conservation programs, field service requests, emergency alerts, public notices, and board documents.

Integration ready

Prepare for existing systems.

Designed for future connections with billing platforms, payment processors, GIS systems, outage tools, meter data, CRM systems, document storage, email/SMS, and work order software.
