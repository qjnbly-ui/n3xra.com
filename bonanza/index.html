Bonanza stays in control

Each new system is explained and scoped before it is activated. The Town decides what to add, when to add it, and which month-to-month services remain useful.

How is this different from a standard municipal website package?

CivicPlus and similar vendors offer mature suites of connected municipal products. N3XRA’s approach is a Bonanza-specific environment where the public website and administrative tools are designed together, supported locally, and expanded around the Town’s actual workflow.

N3XRA will remain the builder and will create the needed systems or connect dependable integrations when that is the stronger solution. A qualified specialist may review, test, audit, or certify work when a grant, law, insurer, or security standard requires independent expertise.
