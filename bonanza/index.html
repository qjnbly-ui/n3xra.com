What I am building toward

The direction I have in mind

This is the longer-term direction I am designing for, so the initial website will not have to be replaced as the Town moves into grant and operational work.

Cybersecurity grant phase

.gov and DNS security, identity and MFA, secure email, immutable backup strategy, recovery testing, policies, documentation, and independent specialists where required.

Additional municipal tools

Agenda and meeting tools, expanded records workflows, resident services, and other modules can be scoped separately as priorities become clear.

Why consider this instead of a standard municipal website package?

CivicPlus and similar vendors provide mature municipal product suites. N3XRA’s advantage here is a smaller, locally supported system shaped around Bonanza’s actual workflow, with dedicated Town data and direct access to the person building it.

This proposal does not claim the full CivicPlus suite already exists inside N3XRA. Specialized grant, security, legal, records, or testing work may involve qualified third parties. The goal is to build the website foundation so those specialists can add to it without forcing the Town to start over.
