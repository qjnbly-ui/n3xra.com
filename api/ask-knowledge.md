## Website request, proposal, and project workflow

- The website request at /website-request collects contact information, project type, goals, audience, requested pages, requested features, budget, target launch date, additional context, and an optional referral code.
- Suggested pages and features are selectable, and a client can add their own ideas.
- Before submission, the review step organizes the request, confirms what was captured, provides useful context, and asks only necessary follow-up questions in a structured form.
- N3XRA reviews a submitted request before preparing a proposal.
- A proposal can describe objectives, scope, deliverables, exclusions, revisions, schedule, one-time items, recurring services, discounts, deposit expectations, payment schedule, and terms.
- A proposal is not a bill. Sending it creates no payment obligation by itself. The client can approve it, request changes, or decline it, and the response is recorded against that exact version.
- Proposals and client responses are available in the signed-in dashboard. If a proposal is missing or appears wrong, use /support.
- After approval, the project can move through agreement, billing, onboarding, production, review, launch, and ongoing management.
- The Website Client Portal at /client-portal organizes each selected website's overview, services and ownership, progress, proposals, onboarding, files and assets, billing, and support.
- Clients can upload website images, logos, documents, icons, and proposed replacements as private drafts for review. Approved files can become published versions. Unused unpublished drafts can be removed.
- During onboarding, clients should never paste passwords into the form. When access to a domain or outside provider is needed, N3XRA uses delegated access or a provider invitation.

## Website billing

- Website billing begins only after the client approves a proposal and N3XRA prepares billing from that accepted proposal.
- Preparing billing creates a fixed billing snapshot of the accepted proposal so later edits do not silently change the approved financial terms.
- The client then completes secure payment setup through Stripe. Depending on the accepted proposal, initial billing can include a deposit or build amount, the first monthly service payment or annual prepaid service, and approved domain or third-party items.
- A client's website billing account is created automatically when billing setup begins. The client does not need to create a separate Stripe account.
- If the portal says billing is not prepared, the approved proposal has not yet been converted into a billing setup.
- If no Stripe billing account exists yet, billing has not reached the secure setup step. It is created automatically when checkout begins.
- If payment setup is required, the billing account exists but a usable payment method has not been completed.
- Verified payment confirmation activates billing; returning from a browser payment page alone does not mark a payment complete.
- Each managed website has its own service subscription, even when one client owns multiple websites.
- The client billing page at /client-portal/billing shows the selected website, service plan, billing interval, subscription status, renewal or commitment dates when available, initial amount, outstanding balance, a safe card summary, and recent invoices.
- Payment details are handled by Stripe. N3XRA does not store full card numbers.
- “Complete secure billing setup” opens payment setup when action is required. “Manage billing in Stripe” lets a connected client update billing details and payment methods, view invoices, and manage allowed cancellation options.
- Later approved work can be invoiced separately. A charge outside the accepted proposal requires a documented client approval reference before it is issued.

## N3XRA AI receptionist

- N3XRA's phone receptionist answers questions about the platform, can provide secured account assistance after caller recognition and keypad PIN verification, can send requested transactional texts to opted-in callers, and may offer an approved live transfer.
- Spoken answers use sentence-sized playback and are designed to tolerate natural pauses and short conversational backchannels. When a caller interrupts, the receptionist tracks what was actually heard; if the caller says “continue,” it resumes with the remaining portion instead of repeating the whole answer.

## N3XRA Records

- N3XRA Records is a searchable records and document platform for private organizational libraries and intentionally published public records. Learn more at /records.
- It supports file uploads and batch imports, metadata, keyword and date search, previews, downloads, editable documents and templates, generated PDFs, contacts, public records pages and embeds, shared libraries, role-based permissions, and meeting-note workflows.
- Supported record workflows include PDFs, DOCX, text, Markdown, CSV, JSON, HTML, and stored scans. Selectable document text can be searchable; scanned material may require OCR before its contents can be searched or edited.
- Meeting-note tools include browser recording or audio upload, playback, transcript processing, saved notes, AI-assisted review and drafting, retry when processing fails, and deletion when the user's role permits it.
- New meeting transcripts use speaker detection by default. Without enrolled voices they use generic labels such as Speaker 1 and Speaker 2. Optional, consent-based voice profiles can replace confident matches with workspace members' names while unknown or uncertain speakers stay generic.
- Workspace members enroll their own voice from Voice Profiles. N3XRA stores the resulting voiceprint for identification and does not retain the raw enrollment recording. Account administrators can disable speaker detection for future meetings in AI Settings.
- Authorized editors can open the branded speaker-correction window, play a short excerpt from each detected speaker's existing meeting audio, and replace that speaker label throughout the saved transcript and linked document.
- Authorized account administrators can invite another organization to accept a complete record packet containing its recording, transcript, notes, references, and generated documents. The recipient must accept the transfer, and existing share links for that packet are revoked when ownership changes.
- Records are private by default. Public pages and embeds show only material deliberately marked public in a library with public access enabled.
- Owners, account administrators, editors, and viewers have different permissions.
- N3XRA administers the service by default, not the customer's private content. Standard support can see account identity, plan, limits, usage totals, and sanitized system health without seeing document names, filenames, recordings, transcripts, private contacts, or file contents.
- A customer can grant temporary, scoped N3XRA support access for documents, recordings and transcripts, downloads, or changes. Grants expire automatically, can be revoked immediately, default to read-only unless broader permission is explicitly granted, and create a customer-visible audit trail.
- Records billing is handled through its own product billing flow. N3XRA does not store full payment-card details.
- Records Free is $0 and includes 25 private documents, one user, 1 GB of storage, and 20 Records AI requests each month.
- Records Starter is $12 per month or $115 per year. It includes 1,000 private documents, one user, 10 GB of storage, and 300 Records AI requests each month.
- Records Organization is $39 per month or $375 per year. It includes 10,000 private documents, up to 15 users, 50 GB of storage, 1,500 Records AI requests each month, shared libraries, invite codes, and public records publishing and embed tools.

## Partner programs

- The public partner page is /partners. Applications are reviewed; applying does not guarantee approval.
- Approved website referral partners can earn $100 when a new, eligible referral purchases a qualifying N3XRA website build and pays for at least one full year of qualifying website service.
- A referral code does not by itself guarantee the $100 commission. Attribution, cleared payments, eligibility, refund and chargeback rules, and continued compliance still apply.
- Approved website referral commissions are generally scheduled for payment within 30 days after all qualification requirements and required payout or tax information are complete.
- Self-referrals, referrals between accounts controlled by the same person or business, and retroactive claims are not eligible unless N3XRA approves them in writing.
- Approved partners may separately participate in software commission programs. Unless product-specific written terms say otherwise, the recurring rate is 20%, increases to 25% after 25 active qualifying referrals, and increases to 30% after 50.
- Software commissions are calculated from Commissionable Net Revenue as defined in the Partner Program Terms. The flat $100 website referral is separate and is not calculated from Commissionable Net Revenue.
- Approved partner accounts receive a partner portal at /client-portal/partners. A partner can choose an available referral code once; after it is created, it cannot be edited. The portal shows balances and referral and commission history.
- Partners cannot promise unauthorized pricing, discounts, results, approval, earnings, delivery dates, or features on N3XRA's behalf.
- Full partner rules are at /partners/terms. The binding Change of Control document for applicable recurring software partner plans is at /partners/change-of-control. It does not apply to the flat $100 website referral unless separate written terms incorporate it.
