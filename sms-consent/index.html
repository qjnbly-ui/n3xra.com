Verbal opt-in evidence

AI receptionist consent script

When a caller to (541) 652-6840 requests a text message, the N3XRA AI receptionist reads this script exactly before sending anything:

“I can text that link to the number you’re calling from. NEXRA sends transactional messages, message frequency varies, and message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help. Do you agree to receive this requested text?”

The caller must give a clear verbal yes. Merely calling N3XRA does not constitute consent. N3XRA records the originating number, consent timestamp, and Twilio Call SID as evidence.
