An open application

We are not asking you to fit into one narrow box.

N3XRA builds websites, software, automation, AI systems, and the business infrastructure around them. That means valuable contributors may come from technical, creative, operational, financial, or relationship-focused backgrounds.

You do not need to answer every optional question. The more perspective you share, the easier it is to understand the role you envision and whether there may be a fit now or later.

Possible paths

A company needs more than one kind of builder.

These are directions, not a list of guaranteed openings. If your strongest contribution falls between them, tell us what we missed.

Build

Technology and product

Software development, website development, product design, AI and automation, technical leadership, research, and implementation.

Grow

Business and market

Business development, sales, partnerships, marketing, communications, content, social media, and community relationships.

Operate

Clients and company

Operations, project delivery, client success, support, finance, administration, documentation, and process improvement.

Shape

Leadership and ownership

Company leadership, strategic advising, investment, internships, apprenticeships, and roles that have not been defined yet.
