N3XRA Admin Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 8, 2026
Application: N3XRA Admin
Android package: com.n3xra.admin

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how N3XRA LLC, registered in Oregon, collects, uses, stores, and shares information through the N3XRA Admin mobile application (the “App”). The App is a private administrative companion for authorized N3XRA platform administrators. Other N3XRA websites, products, and applications may have separate privacy policies describing their own data practices.

2. Information We Collect

  • Authentication information, including the email address and password submitted to Supabase Auth at sign-in. N3XRA Admin does not store or have access to the password in plain text.
  • Administrator account information, including the authenticated user identifier, email address, platform-administrator role, access status, and authentication session data.
  • Administrative notification information, including notification title, summary, priority, product, action link, creation date, and read status. A notification may contain business, account, or contact information depending on the underlying platform event.
  • Push-notification information, including the administrator user identifier, device platform, Expo push token, registration time, last-seen time, and whether a token has been disabled.
  • Basic technical information processed by infrastructure providers, such as IP address, request time, operating-system platform, response status, and error details needed to deliver, secure, and troubleshoot the App.

3. Device Permissions

On Android, the App requests permission to send notifications. You may deny or revoke this permission in device settings. Denying notification permission prevents push alerts but does not prevent an authorized administrator from signing in and viewing the notification inbox.

The current App does not request access to precise or approximate location, contacts, calendars, photos, files, camera, microphone, call logs, SMS content, nearby devices, or an advertising identifier.

4. How We Use Information

  • To authenticate the administrator and verify platform-administrator authorization.
  • To display operational notifications and synchronize their read status.
  • To register an authorized device and deliver requested push notifications.
  • To secure the App, investigate misuse, maintain reliability, and troubleshoot errors.
  • To comply with legal obligations and protect N3XRA, its users, and its services.

5. How Information Is Shared

We do not sell personal information processed through the App. We share limited information with service providers only as needed to operate the App:

  • Supabase provides authentication, database access, server functions, and related infrastructure.
  • Expo provides the push-token and push-notification delivery services used by the App.
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging and Google Play services support notification delivery on Android devices.
  • Apple Push Notification service may support notification delivery if an iOS version of the App is used.

We may also disclose information when required by law, to respond to valid legal process, to protect rights or safety, or in connection with a corporate transaction subject to appropriate confidentiality and legal protections.

6. Advertising and Marketing

The App does not display third-party advertising, use personal information for targeted advertising, or share App data with third parties for their independent marketing or promotional purposes.

7. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect information handled by the App. These safeguards include encrypted network transmission, authenticated sessions, platform-administrator authorization checks, and database access controls. Administrative notification records use row-level access rules that restrict access to authorized platform administrators.

No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Data Retention

  • Administrator account and authorization records are retained while the account remains active and as needed for security, audit, dispute resolution, or legal obligations.
  • Administrative notifications and read status are retained until deleted or archived by an authorized administrator or no longer needed for operational, security, audit, or legal purposes.
  • Push tokens and related device registration data are retained while needed to deliver notifications and may be disabled or deleted when a token becomes invalid, the device is removed, the account is deleted, or deletion is requested.
  • Limited information may remain temporarily in backups, security logs, or provider systems before deletion or isolation under applicable retention schedules.

9. Your Choices and Deletion Requests

You may sign out of the App, revoke notification permission in device settings, or uninstall the App. Uninstalling removes the App from the device but does not by itself delete the administrator account or server-side operational records.

The App does not permit users to create an account. N3XRA provisions and manages administrator accounts separately. To request removal of a device push token, deletion of an administrator account, or access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information, email support@n3xra.com. We may need to verify your identity and authority before completing a request, and we may retain information when legally permitted or required.

10. Children

The App is a restricted business administration tool and is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly authorize children to use it. If you believe a child has provided information through the App, contact us so we can review and delete it where appropriate.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App, its providers, or legal requirements change. The current version will remain available at this URL, and the effective date will be revised when material changes are made.

12. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy or the N3XRA Admin application, contact N3XRA LLC at support@n3xra.com.
